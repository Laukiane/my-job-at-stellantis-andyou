01 / A little about me

One person.
Many hats.
One clear focus.

I’m a Marketing Assistant Apprentice at SLSDPR, a Lille-based automotive parts distributor serving independent repairers, body shops and dealerships.

As the company’s sole marketing assistant, I take projects from first conversation to finished campaign. That means listening closely to professional customers, finding the right practical solution, coordinating people and suppliers, then following through.

I’d love to bring that hands-on, customer-minded experience to Stellantis &You—and grow with a team that knows the automotive business from the inside out.
